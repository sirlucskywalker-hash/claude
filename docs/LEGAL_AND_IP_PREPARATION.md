## Protection map

| Asset | Possible protection | Limits / next evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Original human-authored source and screen expression | Copyright; registration for qualifying version | Not ideas, algorithms, functions or systems; identify human authorship and exclusions |
| Brand name and original logo | Trademark, plus copyright for qualifying artwork | Requires clearance, actual claimant and accurate goods/services; no universal ownership of words |
| Private backend implementation and business know-how | Trade-secret controls and confidentiality agreements | Publicly disclosed material cannot simply become secret again |
| Contractor contributions | Written assignment plus confidentiality and license review | Payment or GitHub access alone does not establish ownership |
| Truly novel technical invention | Patent eligibility/novelty review if justified | No patentability claimed; public disclosure and filing deadlines need counsel review |
| Customer media/data | Privacy, permissions and limited processing license | Customer content is not automatically company-owned |

No combination guarantees that ideas cannot be independently implemented or that copying will never occur. Enforcement requires evidence and proportionate action.

Confirm exact claimant legal name and address, author identities/citizenship, title/version, creation year, publication status/date/country, prior registrations, transfers and any work-for-hire basis. “Apex Training Systems” in Stripe is not proof of a legal entity or ownership. Build an authorship inventory: each original module, UI text, visual asset and document; human contributor; evidence of original expression or creative edits; AI involvement; third-party source/license; signed assignment if needed. Much recent code was generated with AI. Do not claim all generated output as human-authored. Prompts and commit names alone do not establish copyrightable authorship. Disclose and exclude more-than-minimal AI-generated material as required, while identifying qualifying human modifications/selection/arrangement.

After the inventory, freeze an exact release and source hashes; prepare the deposit under Circular 61 for that specific version. Review preexisting material and trade-secret redaction rules before uploading. Never include credentials, customer data or third-party code as claimed original authorship. Do not fabricate an unqualified copyright notice naming an unverified owner.

## Zero-cost controls to complete now / at cutover

Preserve repository history and release hashes. Keep credentials and proprietary backend operations off public artifacts. Maintain least-privilege collaborators and written contributor assignments. Inventory dependency and asset licenses before distributing. Move GitHub private only after Cloudflare deployment, automatic builds and auth callbacks are verified; previous public history/forks cannot be recalled and browser-delivered JavaScript remains inspectable. GitHub public-repository terms permit viewing/forking subject to those terms: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service . Do not imply that making a repository private retroactively removes those permissions.

Keep first-use evidence, contributor agreements and registration records in private business records. For suspected infringement preserve URLs, dates, original/copy comparisons and ownership evidence before requesting removal. Do not automate unverified DMCA claims or assert ownership of functional ideas or licensed dependencies.
